    Why Advocate exists

    Understand the words. Keep the commitments.

    Advocate helps renters navigate important landlord conversations across languages without pretending to be a lawyer.

    1. 1
      Listen and translate

      One control hears either side and translates the conversation.

    2. 2
      Surface what matters

      Gemini flags candidate money terms, penalties, restrictions and vague commitments.

    3. 3
      Keep the promises

      Specific commitments remain visible while the conversation continues.

    4. 4
      Approve the record

      A bilingual summary is prepared only after the renter asks for it.

    Built with Gemini

    Speech transcription, translation, structured review cues and summaries.

    Human stays in control

    Flags are candidates, not legal conclusions. Nothing is automatically sent or signed.
